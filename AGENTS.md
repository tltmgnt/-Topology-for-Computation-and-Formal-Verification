# Project working agreement

Carry clear authorized work through implementation and verification. Preserve exact
finite-state semantics, explicit assumptions, and separate certificate checking.
Never turn valid counterexamples into passing properties by silently excluding
transitions or adding fairness assumptions. Keep certificate validity, overall
obligation satisfaction and real-program correctness distinct. Run unit tests,
exhaustive small-model checks and isolated installed-package workflows after
relevant changes. Only synthetic models may be uploaded by CI. Preserve the license
and do not imply authenticated provenance or independent certification.
